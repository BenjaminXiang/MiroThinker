## Notes

- Debt IDs use a `<change-or-capability>-<seq>` form. Each distinct capability namespace starts seq at 001 (e.g. `paper-prd-config-surface-001` and `paper-prd-source-list-stale-001` are independent capabilities, both starting at 001).
- Statuses:
  - `open` — debt acknowledged; no specific change yet bundles it.
  - `superseded-by-change` — debt is bundled into a specific OpenSpec change cited in the Resolution Plan; will move to Resolved when that change archives. Until then the debt is still active. Introduced 2026-05-10 with `resolve-professor-canonical-baseline`.
  - `grandfathered` — acknowledged but excluded from action by an explicit boundary (typically Phase 0). Distinct from `open` and `wontfix`.
  - `resolved` — change has shipped and the debt is closed.
- Cross-domain entries (e.g. `shared-spec-run-id-on-released-dto-001`) are filed once and may be marked with the originating audit. Per-domain duplication is avoided unless the resolution must be domain-specific.
- Per CLAUDE.md §14 / §14.6, `paper-companion-design-relationship-001` and the new Paper entries `paper-prd-source-list-stale-001` + `paper-prd-msd-phase-a-rule-stale-001` are co-scoped: when the companion-design change is opened, it must reconcile both source-list and Phase-A scope rule items in the same change. See the audit doc cited in their Resolution Plan.
- Provenance for the eleven entries added 2026-05-10:
  - `docs/audits/company-requirement-code-reconciliation-2026-05-10.md` — 5 entries (4 Company + 1 cross-domain)
  - `docs/audits/paper-requirement-code-reconciliation-2026-05-10.md` — 5 entries
  - `docs/audits/patent-requirement-code-reconciliation-2026-05-10.md` — 1 entry
- Acceptance-gap items (e.g. coverage / accuracy targets not yet met, identity-gate precision unverified, BL-Company-001..003 / BL-Paper-001..004 / BL-Patent-001..002 / BL-Professor-001..003 from `docs/data-agent-domain-index.md`) are deliberately excluded from this register. They belong to each PRD's acceptance section.
